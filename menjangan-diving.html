Diving in Menjangan Island

Menjangan Island is one of North Bali's well-known underwater destinations. Its clear water, coral formations and wall-diving scenery make it an exciting area to explore beneath the surface. Balifornia helps visitors arrange diving adventures from the Pemuteran and Gerokgak area.
